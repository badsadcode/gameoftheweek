How The Show Works

A weekly ritual. Mostly safe.

1

The ideas drop

Tom (and Noor) announce this week's bad ideas. One game, two games, who knows. Sage nods calmly while panicking internally.

2

Build week

Ideas get pitched, the right tool for the job gets picked (any engine, any genre), a game gets made and a robot gets hired to test it. Every mishap goes into the devlog.

3

Friday premiere

Tom plays them live on YouTube. Chat watches. We find out together if they're fun.

4

Onto the shelf

The games join the archive with their stream replays, downloadable forever. Then new ideas drop.
