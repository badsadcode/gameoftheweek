Week 1 · Survival on a train

Frostline: Last Train to Haven

The last train crosses 100 km of frozen wasteland. Shovel coal into the furnace for speed and into the stove to keep eight passengers alive. Raiders chase on sleds, and coal never lasts.

Download for Windows Play in browser (experimental) .love file

Windows: unzip, then run Frostline.exe. Controls: A/D move, W/S ladders, Space jump, E interact, J or click to attack, S to duck on roofs.
